About TRACEWORK

Make the interval between words and design decisions discussable.

TRACEWORK is a design-reasoning prototype for architects and designers. It does not decide what a design term means. It helps a designer externalise an interpretation, trace what grounds it, and see the spatial consequences that follow.

01

The problem

Design briefs contain language that can support multiple readings. Designers also often act as though those readings are obvious or shared. TRACEWORK makes that interpretive interval visible without trying to eliminate ambiguity.

02

The method

Reasoning is structured through six linked families: linguistic input, interpretation, grounding, spatial consequence, evaluation and goal. Paths can branch, remain provisional, be compared, revised or rejected.

03

The stance

The tool is provocative with diagnostic support. It is designed to help designers ask better questions and communicate their reasoning — not to prescribe a single correct answer.

Reasoning Frameworks

One project, several ways to read the reasoning.

TRACEWORK Grammar is the default operational model. The six literature frameworks below are optional lenses: they reorganise, prompt and classify the same underlying reasoning without replacing it.

Choose any framework to open a four-part guided explanation, including its architectural example and source text.

Provenance & Evidence

Where did this reasoning come from?

TRACEWORK distinguishes Entities (thoughts and source records), Agents (people, organisations, or software responsible), and Activities (how a thought was authored, observed, interpreted, revised, or generated). Supporting sources are recorded separately from authorship. An unattributed source document is not automatically treated as a person’s statement.

This approach is conceptually informed by the W3C PROV-O provenance ontology ↗: a reasoning statement is recorded as an Entity; attribution identifies an Agent, generation identifies an Activity, and derivation can identify a separate source Entity. TRACEWORK currently stores accessible local metadata, not a formally validated PROV-O/RDF representation.

Select a node → Explain this to inspect its reasoning role and evidence context; choose Edit to record its evidence basis and source citation. Select a wire to keep the map visible while optionally expanding Explain This to investigate a relationship or Gap in Reasoning.
