# TRACEWORK — Research Foundations

TRACEWORK is a research-led architectural reasoning prototype investigating how writing and language can operate as an explicit, inspectable design medium. It makes the interpretive interval between ambiguous briefs and material/spatial decisions easier to discuss, compare and critique.

## Position and limits

The operating sequence is a **design-reasoning grammar**, not a universal cognitive law: Input, Interpretation, Grounding, Spatial Consequence, Evaluation and Goal. Designers may enter, skip or revisit these roles. Externalising a reason does not mean the reason was historically present before the designer articulated it.

Frameworks available in the current website:

1. **TRACEWORK Grammar** — the project's own operational six-role system.
2. **Freeform / Low-Structure** — an interface mode, not a scholarly framework.
3. **Requirements Elicitation** — TRACEWORK adaptation informed by van Lamsweerde.
4. **Five Whys** — an iterative question-asking method.
5. **Laddering / Means–End** — relationships among attribute, consequence and value.
6. **Design Framing** — TRACEWORK reading of framing informed by Dorst.
7. **Reflective Practice** — TRACEWORK adaptation of Schön's reflective design reasoning.
8. **Design Semantics** — design/meaning relations, informed by Krippendorff.

The UI's role sets are TRACEWORK's **operationalisations** of the sources, not quotations from formal taxonomies. Framework switching must not rewrite historical evidence or author identity. See the About page and [`data/references.json`](../data/references.json) for linked publications and the site's framework walkthroughs.

## Provenance and evidence

The interface is conceptually informed by the [W3C PROV-O recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/): **Entities** are recorded thoughts/documents/records, **Agents** are responsible persons/organisations/software, and **Activities** describe generation, observation, revision or analysis. Their relationships support attribution questions, not claims of truth or documentary completeness.

A provenance mismatch is **not** the same as a gap in reasoning. Mixed origins can be intentionally connected. A software-generated suggestion must not be treated as an independent human observation solely because it has been connected to a recorded statement. Supporting records should be identifiable and not invented.

The site does **not** currently export validated RDF/OWL PROV-O. Historical metadata may use legacy identifiers; the UI should invite review instead of silently reassigning authorship.

## Architectural support

Spatial Reading Studio is a curated vocabulary with diagrammatic explanations and selected case studies. Drawings are schematic, not scale-validated construction documents. Links to general literature/search sites may be open-ended **search suggestions**, not verified paper citations. Built-project precedents should be distinguished from conceptual feature examples.

## Empirical context

The repository contains example project JSON and pilot-informed support tables used during development and testing. These datasets are not generic users' autobiographical evidence, nor should a source pilot be assumed familiar to a first-time user. Specific cases and observed patterns can contextualise an investigation, but **frequency of a category transition never proves that two current node statements form a valid reasoning relationship**.

This document describes the project's research framing; it does not replace an academic paper, complete bibliography or full methods account.
